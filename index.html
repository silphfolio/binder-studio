How to use this page
Page format
Pick the binder page you are filling. A 4×3 page holds 12 cards; a 3×3 page holds 9, and the left and right versions are mirror images of each other, so choose the one matching the side of the binder you are laying out.
Dropdowns
Narrow the results. The lists update each other, so an option only appears if it can still work alongside your other choices.
Reset
Puts every dropdown back to Any and shows the full set again.
Pocket bar
Shows how much of the page your current choices take up, and how much is still free.
Colours
Yellow blocks are Pokémon cards, blue blocks are art inserts. Dashed lines mark the binder stitching.
Opening a layout
Click anywhere on a layout, or its Use button, to open it in the Visualise tab and drop your own images in.
Shuffle order
Reorders the results at random, so you see a spread of the whole set rather than working through it from the start. Reset restores the sorted order.
Scrolling
More layouts load automatically as you scroll. Use Load more if they do not appear.

How to use this page
Add images
Click the dotted box to choose files, or drag images onto it. They stay in your browser and are never uploaded anywhere.
Place an image
Tap an image in the tray, then tap a pocket. On a computer you can also drag straight from the tray onto a pocket.
fill / fit / warp
The small badge on a filled pocket. Tap it to change just that image: fill crops it to cover the pocket, fit shrinks the whole image inside, warp stretches it to the exact shape.
Fit dropdown
Sets that same choice for every image on the page at once.
Selecting pockets
Tap a pocket to select it, tap again to deselect. The buttons under the page show what you can do with the current selection.
Swap two pockets
Select two pockets of the same size and press Swap, or just drag one filled pocket onto another. Pockets of different sizes will not swap.
Merge
Select two or four art inserts sitting side by side, then press Merge to join them into a single 1×2, 2×1 or 2×2 insert. Cards cannot be merged, and nothing can merge across the stitch line.
Unmerge
Select a large art insert and press Unmerge to break it back into single pockets.
× on a pocket
Empties that pocket. The image stays in your tray.
× on a tray image
Removes the image completely, including from any pocket it was in.
‹ Layout / Layout ›
Moves through the layouts left by your Browse filters. Your images move with you.
Shuffle
Jumps to a random layout. Images stay placed, sliding to the nearest matching pocket.
Clear slots
Empties the whole page at once, keeping your images in the tray.
Download PNG
Saves the page at true print size: every single pocket comes out 63 × 88 mm, matching a real card. Pick 300 dpi for printing, or 150 dpi for a smaller file to post online.

Your images

Add scans or photos, then tap an image and tap a pocket — or drag one onto a pocket. Images are cropped to fill, and stay put when you shuffle to another layout.
