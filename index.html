Silphfolio's Binder Layout Generator

One page, endless possibilities. Mix cards and art inserts until it feels right, then see it with your own images.

How to use this page
Page format
Pick the binder page you are filling. A 4×3 page holds 12 cards; a 3×3 page holds 9, and the left and right versions are mirror images of each other, so choose the one matching the side of the binder you are laying out.
Dropdowns
Narrow the results. The number beside each choice is how many layouts you'd be left with, and the lists update each other so an option only appears if it can still work.
Reset
Puts every dropdown back to Any and shows the full set again.
Pocket bar
Shows how much of the page your current choices take up, and how much is still free.
Colours
Yellow blocks are Pokémon cards, blue blocks are art inserts. Dashed lines mark the binder stitching.
Use →
Opens that layout in the Visualise tab so you can drop your own images into it.
Previous / Next
Steps through the results, 72 layouts at a time.

How to use this page
Add images
Click the dotted box to choose files, or drag images onto it. They stay in your browser and are never uploaded anywhere.
Place an image
Tap an image in the tray, then tap a pocket. On a computer you can also drag straight from the tray onto a pocket.
fill / fit / warp
The small badge on a filled pocket. Tap it to change just that image: fill crops it to cover the pocket, fit shrinks the whole image inside, warp stretches it to the exact shape.
Fit dropdown
Sets that same choice for every image on the page at once.
× on a pocket
Empties that pocket. The image stays in your tray.
× on a tray image
Removes the image completely, including from any pocket it was in.
‹ Layout / Layout ›
Moves through the layouts left by your Browse filters. Your images move with you.
Shuffle
Jumps to a random layout. Images stay placed, sliding to the nearest matching pocket.
Clear slots
Empties the whole page at once, keeping your images in the tray.
Download PNG
Saves the page exactly as shown, ready to post.

Your images

Add scans or photos, then tap an image and tap a pocket — or drag one onto a pocket. Images are cropped to fill, and stay put when you shuffle to another layout.
